Fast Booking.
Anywhere. Anytime.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Is Softifex cloud-based or installed locally?
Softifex is a web-based ERP — accessible from any browser. It can be deployed on your own server (on-premise) or hosted in the cloud depending on your preference and infrastructure setup.
Does it support mobile booking for salesmen?
Yes. Softifex includes a dedicated fast web-based mobile booking app for salesmen. It works offline, syncs in real time, and supports area-wise routing, customer visits, live stock checks, and order placement.
Can it manage batch-wise inventory and expiry?
Absolutely. Batch-wise stock tracking with FEFO/FIFO support, expiry date alerts, near-expiry reports, and batch hold management are core features — essential for pharmaceutical compliance.
Does Softifex include full accounting?
Yes. The accounting module covers double-entry bookkeeping, chart of accounts, journal vouchers, cash/bank management, trial balance, P&L statement, and balance sheet — plus GST-ready tax reporting.
Is customization available for specific workflows?
Yes. Techstersol provides custom development for unique business workflows, custom reports, and integrations. Enterprise plan clients get fully tailored solutions as part of the package.
Do you provide training and ongoing support?
All plans include comprehensive onboarding training for your team. Ongoing support is available via WhatsApp, phone, and email. Enterprise clients receive dedicated support with priority response SLAs.
