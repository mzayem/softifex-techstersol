Security and control

Your data stays yours, and stays safe

  • Role-based accessSet every page to None, View, Create, Update or Full. Choose which dashboard cards each role sees.
  • Protected sign-inAccount lockout after repeated wrong attempts, with managed sessions.
  • Encrypted backupsManual or automatic daily, every 3 days or weekly, with your choice of retention, and easy restore.
  • Tamper alertsAdministrators are warned when security records need attention, with a recovery console to repair them.
  • Ledgers for auditData, sales and stock ledgers trace every entry.
