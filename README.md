# Softifex ERP

![Softifex ERP](assets/img/Softifex-logo.png)

## Complete Pharmaceutical ERP Solution

Softifex ERP is a modern web-based Pharmaceutical ERP platform developed by **Techstersol**. The system is designed for pharmaceutical distributors, wholesalers, medical stores, and inventory-driven businesses that require complete control over inventory, sales, purchases, accounting, reporting, and mobile order booking.

---

## Features

### Inventory Management

- Batch-wise stock tracking
- Expiry date management
- Real-time inventory monitoring
- Multi-location stock control
- Product categorization

### Sales Management

- Sales invoices
- Customer management
- Credit and cash sales
- Sales returns
- Area-wise sales tracking

### Purchase Management

- Purchase orders
- Vendor management
- Purchase returns
- Cost tracking
- Supplier ledger

### Accounting

- General ledger
- Accounts receivable
- Accounts payable
- Profit & Loss reports
- Financial statements

### Reporting

- Inventory reports
- Sales reports
- Purchase reports
- Customer reports
- Vendor reports
- Profitability analysis
- Tax and GST reports

### Mobile Booking Application

- Area-wise salesman booking
- Real-time order placement
- Customer visit tracking
- Offline booking support
- Instant synchronization

### Security

- Role-based access control
- User management
- Activity monitoring
- Secure authentication
